# Clinical Reference Register — Six Category-Specific Variants

This register identifies authoritative references consulted when drafting the six category-specific clinical variants in `src/questionBank.ts`. These references support the clinical concepts described; they do **not** constitute item-level clinical sign-off. A qualified reviewer must verify the exact claims, local protocol implications, and answer defensibility before publication.

| Variant | Reference | Supported content |
|---|---|---|
| Newborn thermoregulation | [AHA Neonatal Resuscitation Guidelines, Part 5 (2025)](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/neonatal-resuscitation/) | Thermal protection of preterm newborns; use of warming strategies with temperature monitoring and avoidance of hyperthermia |
| Newborn hypoglycemia | [Canadian Paediatric Society: The screening and management of newborns at risk for low blood glucose](https://cps.ca/en/documents/position/newborns-at-risk-for-low-blood-glucose) | Symptomatic neonatal hypoglycemia requires immediate treatment; IV dextrose is indicated for symptomatic infants, with glucose reassessment |
| Pulmonary embolism after birth | [RCOG Green-top Guideline 37b: Thromboembolic Disease in Pregnancy and the Puerperium](https://www.rcog.org.uk/media/wj2lpco5/gtg-37b-1.pdf) | Assessment and investigation pathway for suspected PE in pregnancy and the puerperium |
| Pulmonary embolism after birth | [RCOG: Diagnosis and treatment of venous thrombosis in pregnancy and after birth](https://www.rcog.org.uk/for-the-public/browse-our-patient-information/diagnosis-and-treatment-of-venous-thrombosis-in-pregnancy-and-after-birth) | Postpartum VTE risk and urgent symptoms of PE, including sudden dyspnea and chest pain |
| Meningococcal meningitis | [CDC: Meningococcal Disease — Infection Control](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/healthcare-personnel-epidemiology-control/meningococcal-disease.html) | Droplet plus standard precautions for known or suspected meningococcal disease; 24-hour effective-therapy interval |

| Autism and care adaptations | [NICE CG170: Autism spectrum disorder in under 19s — support and management](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg170/chapter/recommendations) | Individualized visual supports and adjustments for sensory sensitivities, including noise and the care environment |

## Review cautions

- Neonatal glucose thresholds and treatment pathways differ across jurisdictions and institutions. The item deliberately directs the nurse to the neonatal protocol; the clinical reviewer must confirm the scenario's glucose value and treatment choice against the adopted local standard.
- RSV oxygen thresholds and admission criteria are age- and guideline-dependent. The item presents an infant with SpO2 89%, retractions, tachypnea, poor intake, and reduced urine output; reviewers must confirm the intended threshold and care pathway.
- Droplet precautions for meningococcal disease are not interchangeable with the precautions for every bacterial meningitis organism. The scenario specifically raises concern for meningococcal disease.
- The postpartum PE variant is a recognition-and-escalation item, not a complete diagnostic or anticoagulation order set. Do not infer that a nurse independently initiates anticoagulation.
- The autism variant is individualized to caregiver-reported communication supports; avoid treating all autistic children as having the same sensory or communication profile.
